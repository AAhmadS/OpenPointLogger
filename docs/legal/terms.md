## 2. What Trailmark is

turns a closed topic into a formatted report. It runs on your computer. Your

## 4. Third-party services you choose to enable

This is the part of these terms that matters most, so it is stated plainly.

Trailmark makes no network request of its own accord. It contacts exactly four
kinds of destination, each only when you have enabled the relevant feature and
supplied your own key or token:

| Feature | What is sent, and where |
|---|---|
| **AI assist** (polish, summarise) | The text of the points concerned, to the AI provider you selected — OpenRouter by default, or OpenAI, Mistral, AvalAI or Google AI Studio. |
| **Voice logging** | Recorded audio of your speech, to the transcription endpoint you configured (OpenRouter by default). |
| **Telegram bridge** | Your messages and any files you send the bot, through Telegram's own servers, using a bot token you create and hold. |
| **Citation lookup** | The DOI or identifier you entered, to `doi.org` and Crossref. |
